# Screenshot Checklist

Capture real screenshots only after the app is running with seeded data. Do not add fake images.

Recommended screenshots:

1. Dashboard at `/dashboard`
2. Risk Register at `/risks`
3. Risk Detail at `/risks/:id`
4. Create Risk at `/risks/new`
5. Assets at `/assets`
6. Controls at `/controls`

Place captured images in `docs/screenshots/` and update the README with relative links.
